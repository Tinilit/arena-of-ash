# Game design

The Wanderer begins with 100 gold, a Wooden Sword, Throwing Knife and Throwing Axe. There are no character attributes, levels, experience, allocation points or energy/stamina resource. Every fighter has 100 health, base unarmed attack 12 and fixed movement speeds. Equipment changes attack and armor; the selected hand supplies its own weapon bonus. Armor in helmet/body slots also contributes. The other hand and carried belt items do not stack bonuses.

Contact damage is max(1, round(12 + active gear attack − defender armor)), halved with rounding and a minimum of one while the AI guards. A kick deals zero HP damage and only pushes/staggers or knocks down. There is no random accuracy or passive health regeneration.

Light attacks last 1000 ms, active at 45–67%, followed by 280 ms cooldown for that hand. Both hands can overlap; their contact IDs and cooldowns are independent. Heavy attacks are disabled. F/3 performs a 1050 ms front kick on the ground or in a jump/hop, active at 36–56%, followed by 350 ms cooldown. Only actual selected blade/fist contact damages an opponent, once per hand action. Boot contact applies kick reactions without HP damage. Anticipation, recovery and idle equipment do no damage. Sword grips and spear shafts are excluded from damaging geometry.

A kick hit pushes back and has a 20% chance to knock the opponent down. Knockdown takes 550 ms (and waits for ground contact if airborne), followed by a full 1000 ms on the ground and an 1800 ms supported get-up. These states lock actions and movement without granting invulnerability. Further contacts damage but do not restart the floor transfer. A normal kick staggers for 850 ms.

Walk/run speeds are 110/200 logical units per second. Jump gravity is 1350 units/s² and initial vertical speed is 500 units/s. No mid-air second jump is allowed. Player-only directional hops have their own anticipation, ballistic arc, landing and cooldown. No action consumes a hidden resource. Bounds and separation prevent walking through walls or a standing opponent.

The AI moves into weapon reach, makes light attacks, backs away when crowded, and sometimes guards or performs an ordinary jump. It does not kick or use special hops. Simulation runs at 120 Hz. Simultaneous lethal contacts produce a draw with the defeat payout. Losing focus pauses simulation and clears held input.

Opponents: Arena Novice → Iron Pit Fighter → Veteran Gladiator. Wins advance; the veteran repeats. Each victory increments an uncapped count. At 0–2 victories opponents draw novice weapons (attack 1–4) without armor; 3–5 unlock attack 4–9, leather body armor and a basic helmet; 6+ unlock attack 9+ and the strongest available armor. Dual-wield probability rises from 20% to at most 75%. Generated enemy loadouts use distinct owned weapon IDs. Difficulty comes from equipment rather than attributes. Victory grants 50 gold; defeat grants 10. A new battle starts fully healed. Esc abandons a battle without a reward. Persistent reward IDs prevent duplicate payouts.

The default catalog contains twelve definitions, including two belt throwables and a healing potion. Inventory records ownership; the backpack shows all unequipped items and equipment cards show worn gear. Consumables can accumulate through repeated/bulk purchases. Each slot carries one copy; spare copies remain in the backpack without battle auto-refill. Looted weapon copies also accumulate. There is no selling. Shields remain temporarily absent. Spears use a haft/leaf head and thrust motion. Ash Vanguard and Grounded are the only appearance and motion systems. See [equipment](EQUIPMENT.md).

Q/E uses belt slots; Ctrl+the selected hand attack throws its weapon. Every real throw is overarm and can be airborne. Release spends one copy. Grounded equipment can be recovered with G, including enemy throws; recovery restores a compatible empty slot or backpack ownership. Unrecovered items remain lost on leaving. A potion costs 30 gold and heals up to 35 HP once at the animated sip (55% of 2100 ms); a hit before the sip preserves it. Full-health use is disabled. Camp previews preserve inventory. See [equipment](EQUIPMENT.md).

C starts a 450 ms weapon parry with an immediate 140 ms precision window and 750 ms recovery. A physical frontal blade/fist contact during the window is deflected for zero damage and gives the attacker 1000 ms stun with a short balance recoil. Parry requires a held weapon and no active action/stun/knockdown. Holding/repeated presses cannot extend the window. Kicks and projectiles are not parried. There is no range-only success or invulnerability.

Enemy AI has a separate throw RNG. After at least 1800 ms, an eligible idle armed enemy at 230–600 units can roll a 12% throw chance, at most once per 9000 ms, and may attempt only one throw per fight. An interrupted attempt is not retried. It uses the same overarm release/contact/ownership rules as the player and can leave the enemy unarmed.

Victory grants its reward once, stops combat damage/AI and leaves movement/collection available until Continue. Falling projectiles still land, so a lethal thrown weapon can be collected. Defeat/draw transition automatically; a new match resets health but retains recovered inventory and equipment.
