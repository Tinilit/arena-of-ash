# Repository conventions

- Write source code, identifiers, comments, UI strings, documentation and commit messages in English.
- Converse with the user in Ukrainian unless they request another language.
- Use Ash Vanguard art and Grounded motion only. Do not reintroduce Style Studio, alternate visual styles or paired warglaives unless the user requests them.
- Keep gameplay rules independent of Phaser. Use the existing checkout, Node.js 22 and the commands documented in README.md.

- Keep both legs below the skirt and the free front arm behind the torso and the armed rear arm in the foreground, with their own shoulder attachments. Shields are temporarily omitted from the default catalog and opponent loadouts.

- Front/left and rear/right hands are explicitly usable with LMB/J and RMB/K. Empty hands punch; never substitute another hand's weapon. Keep independent action clocks, cooldowns, animation tracks and contact IDs for each hand; allow simultaneous or staggered attacks. Heavy attacks remain disabled. Kicks are body-exclusive. Q/E uses belt slots: throwables launch from the rear hand; potions play the rear-hand drink action and heal at the sip. Ctrl+the corresponding hand attack throws that hand weapon, including in the air. Actual throws always use the overarm clip. Consume released items permanently and save immediately, including misses/abandoned battles; interrupted wind-ups preserve ownership. Drinking is disabled at full HP, caps healing at 100 and consumes once at the sip. Keep the front/near hand relaxed, with a softly flexed elbow and neutral wrist. Keep arm depth separate from shoulder transforms: front/free behind torso, rear/armed in front. Retain front-hand attack support and explicit hand mounts for future equipment. Camp movement previews must not deal damage or change saved progression. Arena combat is real time: damage requires actual selected blade/fist or flying projectile contact with a body, at most once per swing/projectile; boot contact applies zero-damage reactions. Keep simulation and collision geometry independent of Phaser; read the actual rendered joint transforms for arena hitboxes.

- Keep gameplay and UI coordinates in the 1280×720 logical space. Use the shared `label` factory for text so display density and texture cleanup are managed. Canvas backing dimensions are physical render pixels, not gameplay bounds.

- Item categories and equipment positions are separate. Keep slot compatibility, unique equipped IDs and primary-hand selection in the pure domain layer. There are no attributes, levels, XP or energy/stamina resource; health and active equipment bonuses remain. Do not add hidden resource costs; do not reintroduce player Defense/Recover buttons or bindings. Throw previews must always use the rear hand, must not consume inventory or deal damage. Retain the underarm backswing and the separate overarm volleyball-style cock/extension/release. Keep both on continuous IK branches. Keep the head permanently in the torso plane below the throwing-arm plane; never reparent it over an arm. The front hand temporarily uses the foreground plane during a real front-hand throw and returns behind the torso afterward; shoulder transforms stay fixed.

- Pain/stun interrupts actions. Kicks work on the ground, in ordinary jumps and in directional hops. Air kicks retain horizontal movement/launch velocity, extend the striking leg and keep the other leg tucked. Kicks deal zero HP damage; they push, stagger and have a 20% knockdown chance: 550 ms fall, at least 1000 ms grounded, then 1800 ms supported get-up with locked controls and no jump impulse. Further hits do not restart recovery. Normal kick knockback uses constrained physical displacement and a displacement-driven backwards step cycle. Backpack UI shows every unequipped category and excludes all equipped IDs without deleting ownership.

- Victory count in `progress` is uncapped and drives enemy equipment tiers. Inject a separate RNG for spawn loadouts, AI decisions and kick outcomes. Generate compatible, uniquely equipped catalog items; dual-wielding enemies can use both hands independently.
