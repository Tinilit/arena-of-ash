# Repository conventions

- Write source code, identifiers, comments, UI strings, documentation and commit messages in English.
- Converse with the user in Ukrainian unless they request another language.
- Use Ash Vanguard art and Grounded motion only. Do not reintroduce Style Studio, alternate visual styles or paired warglaives unless the user requests them.
- Keep gameplay rules independent of Phaser. Use the existing checkout, Node.js 22 and the commands documented in README.md.

- Keep both legs below the skirt and the free front arm behind the torso and the armed rear arm in the foreground for one-handed actions, with their own shoulder attachments. During two-handed holds the complete rear arm and elbow stay in front of the torso; the front arm and elbow stay behind it. Relaxed melee idle rests the weapon on the rear shoulder with the front arm hanging freely; combat stance and attacks restore both exact grips. Preserve identical joint transforms when changing their render parents. Shields are temporarily omitted from the default catalog and opponent loadouts.

- Front/left and rear/right hands are explicitly usable with LMB/J and RMB/K. Empty hands punch for exactly 1 HP damage regardless of armor, guard or critical modifiers; never substitute another hand's weapon. Keep independent action clocks, cooldowns, animation tracks and contact IDs for each hand; allow simultaneous or staggered attacks. Two-handed items (`hands: 2`) occupy the rear/right slot and reserve the front/left hand, with one owned/equipped copy. Both attack inputs use the single rear swing and shared recovery; the support hand cannot punch or throw independently. Equipping another front-hand item returns the two-handed weapon to the backpack. Pickup must not displace either occupied hand. Heavy attacks remain disabled. Kicks are body-exclusive. Q/E uses belt slots: throwables launch from the rear hand; potions play the rear-hand drink action and heal at the sip. Ctrl+the corresponding hand attack throws that hand weapon, including in the air. Actual throws always use the overarm clip. Consume exactly one copy from the source slot at release/sip and save immediately; interrupted wind-ups preserve ownership. G collects a nearby grounded projectile once, returns it to an empty compatible slot or the backpack, transfers enemy ownership and saves immediately. Unrecovered items stay lost after leaving. Spare copies never automatically refill a battle slot. Drinking is disabled at full HP, caps healing at 100 and consumes once at the sip. Keep the front/near hand relaxed, with a softly flexed elbow and neutral wrist. Keep arm depth separate from shoulder transforms: front/free behind torso, rear/armed in front. Retain front-hand attack support and explicit hand mounts for future equipment. Camp movement previews must not deal damage or change saved progression. Arena combat is real time: damage requires actual selected blade/fist or flying projectile contact with a body, at most once per swing/projectile; boot contact applies zero-damage reactions. Bound finishers are the explicit guaranteed-execution exception. Keep simulation and collision geometry independent of Phaser; read the actual rendered joint transforms for arena hitboxes.

- Keep gameplay and UI coordinates in the 1280×720 logical space. Use the shared `label` factory for text so display density and texture cleanup are managed. Canvas backing dimensions are physical render pixels, not gameplay bounds.

- Item categories and equipment positions are separate. Keep slot compatibility, owned quantities and primary-hand selection in the pure domain layer. Inventory IDs are unique definitions; optional quantities record total owned copies (missing means one). Each equipment slot holds one copy, and equipped counts must not exceed ownership. Allow repeated/bulk consumable purchases and show only unequipped quantities in the backpack. There are no attributes, levels, XP or energy/stamina resource; health and active equipment bonuses remain. Do not add hidden resource costs; do not reintroduce player Defense/Recover buttons or bindings. Throw previews must always use the rear hand, must not consume inventory or deal damage. Retain the underarm backswing and the separate overarm volleyball-style cock/extension/release. Keep both on continuous IK branches. Keep the head permanently in the torso plane below the throwing-arm plane; never reparent it over an arm. The front hand temporarily uses the foreground plane during a real front-hand throw and returns behind the torso afterward; shoulder transforms stay fixed.

- Pain/stun interrupts actions. Kicks work on the ground, in ordinary jumps and in directional hops. Air kicks retain horizontal movement/launch velocity, extend the striking leg and keep the other leg tucked. Kicks deal zero HP damage; they push, stagger and have a 20% knockdown chance: 550 ms fall, at least 1000 ms grounded, then 1800 ms supported get-up with locked controls and no jump impulse. Further hits do not restart recovery. Normal kick knockback uses constrained physical displacement and a displacement-driven backwards step cycle. Backpack UI shows every unequipped category and excludes all equipped IDs without deleting ownership.

- Victory count in `progress` is uncapped and drives enemy equipment tiers. Inject separate RNGs for spawn loadouts, AI decisions, rare enemy throws and kick outcomes. Generate compatible owned catalog items; dual-wielding enemies can use both hands independently.

- C performs a weapon parry: 140 ms contact window, 450 ms action plus 750 ms recovery, no hold/repeat extension. Only a real frontal melee blade/fist contact can be parried; no range-only hits, kick/projectile immunity or parry damage. A success cancels both attacker hands, breaks balance and applies a full 1000 ms stun, then automatically follows with one open-palm kick-strength shove at 150 ms. The counter-shove deals no HP damage and never rolls knockdown. Misses do not stun. Preserve fixed shoulders and bone lengths.
- H starts a close-range coupled lift, H again queues the throw. Reach can miss or be interrupted; the captive cannot act. During hold the carrier can walk at 80 units/s using A/D, turns with movement and throws in the current facing direction; jumping and other attacks remain locked. Carry the horizontal target above both reachable hands; preserve bone lengths and inventory. A zero-damage throw uses ballistic flight followed by existing heavy floor recovery. Steel Crossbow is a real two-handed catalog weapon with physical swept bolts and automatic reloading. A bolt is not a thrown equipment copy: it does not consume the crossbow or enter inventory through pickup. Rifle remains a harmless keyboard preview.
- Victory leaves the arena open for movement and grounded equipment collection until Continue. Advance falling projectiles without further damage/AI. Grant rewards once on the outcome, persist every collection, and carry recovered equipment into the next battle. Defeat/draw retain their automatic result transition. Enemies may attempt one rare overarm weapon throw per fight using their own RNG; they do not use player parries, pickups, belt items, kicks or forward hops. Enemy backsteps react once per committed player swing, use a separate RNG and a 2200 ms decision cooldown, require room behind, and retain normal physical vulnerability.

- V performs a two-handed weapon shove: an actual rendered grip/haft capsule must touch the opponent. It deals zero HP damage, pushes with the normal kick impulse and causes stagger without knockdown. X starts an armed paired execution against a nearby grounded down target. The actor eases to a calculated socket, stays attached, and reserves the victim until the committed fatal impact at 680 ms. Starting cancels the actor’s prior attack/recovery; input cannot move either participant out of the execution and the victim cannot begin getting up. The committed impact deals the remaining HP once, bypassing armor. Do not revert this to a free-swing collision window. Standing/falling/getting-up targets cannot start an execution; empty hands cannot execute. Preserve fixed bones, continuous weapon angles and exact two-hand grip spacing.

- Camp and Character have no demonstration buttons, and the active arena has no action/movement/belt buttons. Keep normal navigation, equipment/shop controls and post-victory Continue. Combat uses mouse buttons and physical keyboard codes with a static control legend.
