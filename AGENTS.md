# Repository conventions

- Write source code, identifiers, comments, UI strings, documentation and commit messages in English.
- Converse with the user in Ukrainian unless they request another language.
- Use Ash Vanguard art and Grounded motion only. Do not reintroduce Style Studio, alternate visual styles or paired warglaives unless the user requests them.
- Keep gameplay rules independent of Phaser. Use the existing checkout, Node.js 22 and the commands documented in README.md.

- Keep both legs below the skirt and the free front arm behind the torso and the armed rear arm in the foreground for one-handed actions, with their own shoulder attachments. During two-handed holds the complete rear arm and elbow stay in front of the torso; the front arm and elbow stay behind it. Relaxed melee idle rests the weapon on the rear shoulder with the front arm hanging freely; combat stance and attacks restore both exact grips. Preserve identical joint transforms when changing their render parents. Shields are skill-gated rear-hand passive armor. Keep their artwork first in the pelvis container behind every limb/body layer, positioned from the rear-hand transform. They cannot block or bash.

- Front/left and rear/right hands are explicitly usable with LMB/J and RMB/K. Empty hands cannot punch or start light attacks; never substitute another hand's weapon. Kicks and grabs require their learned skills. Keep independent action clocks, cooldowns, animation tracks and contact IDs for each hand; allow simultaneous or staggered attacks. Two-handed items (`hands: 2`) occupy the rear/right slot and reserve the front/left hand, with one owned/equipped copy. Both attack inputs use the single rear swing and shared recovery; the support hand cannot punch or throw independently. Equipping another front-hand item returns the two-handed weapon to the backpack. Pickup must not displace either occupied hand. Heavy attacks remain disabled. Kicks are body-exclusive. Q/E uses belt slots: throwables launch from the rear hand; potions play the rear-hand drink action and heal at the sip. Ctrl+the corresponding hand attack throws that hand weapon, including in the air. Actual throws always use the overarm clip. Consume exactly one copy from the source slot at release/sip and save immediately; interrupted wind-ups preserve ownership. G collects a nearby grounded projectile once, returns it to an empty compatible slot or the backpack, transfers enemy ownership and saves immediately. Unrecovered items stay lost after leaving. Spare copies never automatically refill a battle slot. Healing drinks are disabled at full HP and cap healing at the fighter maximum (100 for the player). Haste can be drunk at full HP; all drinks consume once at the sip. Keep the front/near hand relaxed, with a softly flexed elbow and neutral wrist. Keep arm depth separate from shoulder transforms: front/free behind torso, rear/armed in front. Retain front-hand attack support and explicit hand mounts for future equipment. Camp movement previews must not deal damage or change saved progression. Arena combat is real time: damage requires actual selected blade or flying projectile contact with a body, at most once per swing/projectile; boot contact applies zero-damage reactions. Bound finishers are the explicit guaranteed-execution exception. Keep simulation and collision geometry independent of Phaser; read the actual rendered joint transforms for arena hitboxes.

- Keep gameplay and UI coordinates in the 1280×720 logical space. Use the shared `label` factory for text so display density and texture cleanup are managed. Canvas backing dimensions are physical render pixels, not gameplay bounds.

- Item categories and equipment positions are separate. Keep slot compatibility, owned quantities and primary-hand selection in the pure domain layer. Inventory IDs are unique definitions; optional quantities record total owned copies (missing means one). Each equipment slot holds one copy, and equipped counts must not exceed ownership. New games/reset own only the wooden sword; throwing knives, axes and all potions must be bought. Both belt slots start empty. Backpack Equip fills a free compatible slot before replacing the selected occupied slot; two-handed support slots are reserved. Allow repeated/bulk consumable purchases and show only unequipped quantities in the backpack. There are no attributes, levels, XP or energy/stamina resource; health and active equipment bonuses remain. Do not add hidden resource costs; do not reintroduce player Defense/Recover buttons or bindings. Throw previews must always use the rear hand, must not consume inventory or deal damage. Retain the underarm backswing and the separate overarm volleyball-style cock/extension/release. Keep both on continuous IK branches. Keep the head permanently in the torso plane below the throwing-arm plane; never reparent it over an arm. The front hand temporarily uses the foreground plane during a real front-hand throw and returns behind the torso afterward; shoulder transforms stay fixed.

- Pain/stun interrupts actions. Kicks work on the ground, in ordinary jumps and in directional hops. Air kicks retain horizontal movement/launch velocity, extend the striking leg and keep the other leg tucked. Kicks deal zero HP damage; they push, stagger and have a 20% knockdown chance: 550 ms fall, at least 1000 ms grounded, then 1800 ms supported get-up with locked controls and no jump impulse. Further hits do not restart recovery. Normal kick knockback uses constrained physical displacement and a displacement-driven backwards step cycle. Backpack UI shows every unequipped category and excludes all equipped IDs without deleting ownership.

- Victory count in `progress` is uncapped and drives enemy equipment tiers. Inject separate RNGs for spawn loadouts, AI decisions, rare enemy throws, kick outcomes, parry disarms and each hammer effect. Generate compatible owned catalog items; dual-wielding enemies can use both hands independently.

- C performs a weapon parry: 140 ms contact window, 450 ms action plus 750 ms recovery, no hold/repeat extension. Only real frontal contact can be parried; melee blade contact uses the counter-stun/shove, while crossbow bolts require physical weapon interception inside the same window and deflect in a random direction. No range-only hits, kick immunity or parry damage. Projectile deflection transfers projectile ownership, keeps the bolt dangerous and never remotely stuns, disarms or shoves its shooter. A success cancels both attacker hands, breaks balance and applies a full 1000 ms stun, then follows with an open-palm kick-strength shove at 150 ms unless learned Counterstrike rolls its independent 20% chance: a real weapon counter replaces the shove completely. Roll once per parry, not per contacting hand/bolt; a selected counter must never render or apply the palm shove. The counter-shove deals no HP damage and never rolls knockdown. A successful weapon parry rolls one 20% disarm chance against the contacting hand, at most once per parry. A disarm consumes exactly one equipped copy and drops a harmless spent projectile that can settle and be recovered by either fighter. Misses do not stun. Preserve fixed shoulders and bone lengths.
- H starts a close-range coupled lift, H again queues the throw. Reach can miss or be interrupted; the captive cannot act. During hold the carrier can walk at 80 units/s using A/D, turns with movement and throws in the current facing direction; jumping and other attacks remain locked. Carry the horizontal target above both reachable hands; preserve bone lengths and inventory. A zero-damage throw uses ballistic flight followed by existing heavy floor recovery. Steel Crossbow is a real two-handed catalog weapon with physical swept bolts and automatic reloading. A bolt is not a thrown equipment copy: it does not consume the crossbow or enter inventory through pickup. Rifle remains a harmless keyboard preview.
- Victory leaves the arena open for movement and grounded equipment collection until Continue. Advance falling projectiles without further damage/AI. Grant rewards once on the outcome, persist every collection, and carry recovered equipment into the next battle. Defeat/draw retain their automatic result transition. Production AI for both fighters can use independent hands, timed parries, kicks and jump kicks, both hops, jumps, belt items, rare weapon throws, two-handed shoves, symmetric grabs/throws and armed finishers. Parry decisions have a per-threat 28% chance and timing jitter; never react perfectly to every swing. AI prioritizes its disarmed weapon, predicts the landing point, runs toward it and can kick/grapple an obstructing opponent. Pickups never replace held gear or fill a reserved two-handed support grip. Enemy backsteps react once per committed player swing, use a separate RNG and a 2200 ms decision cooldown, require room behind, and retain normal physical vulnerability.

- V performs a two-handed weapon shove: an actual rendered grip/haft capsule must touch the opponent. It deals zero HP damage, pushes with the normal kick impulse and causes stagger without knockdown. X starts an armed paired execution against a nearby grounded down target. The actor eases to a calculated socket, stays attached, and reserves the victim until the committed fatal impact at 680 ms. Starting cancels the actor’s prior attack/recovery; input cannot move either participant out of the execution and the victim cannot begin getting up. The committed impact deals the remaining HP once, bypassing armor. Do not revert this to a free-swing collision window. Standing/falling/getting-up targets cannot start an execution; empty hands cannot execute. Preserve fixed bones, continuous weapon angles and exact two-hand grip spacing.

- Camp and Character have no demonstration buttons, and the active arena has no action/movement/belt buttons. Keep normal navigation, equipment/shop controls and post-victory Continue. Combat uses mouse buttons and physical keyboard codes with a skill-dependent control legend.

- Unblocked nonlethal melee hammer hits roll independent 10% knockdown and 10% kick-strength push chances, once per physical contact. Both may occur together. Parried hits, misses, blocks, other weapons and existing floor transfers do not roll these effects. Use the existing supported knockdown/get-up and stagger movement. Closed Steel Helm and Steel Cuirass are original Ash Vanguard armor in the Armor shop category.

- Auto Battle starts enabled in every production arena fight. Any keyboard key or mouse button takes manual control and applies that input; mouse movement only aims. B resumes Auto Battle from manual mode (and stops it when active). Keep the DEV-only `manual=1` opt-out for foundation browser fixtures. Auto Battle uses the player's actual equipment and consumables. Show mode and haste countdown, retaining manual navigation and post-victory Continue. Legacy foundation browser fixtures may use the DEV-only `ai=training` policy; production always enables the full AI.
- Potion of Haste grants double actor simulation/animation speed for 10000 real active-combat milliseconds after the sip, without stacking or persistence between fights. Scale movement, action/cooldown/recovery clocks and owned projectile travel together; preserve physics trajectories and exact partial-expiry integration. Never accelerate the opponent or the buff countdown with the drinker.
- Two-handed spears/tridents use a 52-unit support grip and a linear thrust/withdrawal, rather than the sword/hammer overhead arc. The actual tines alone award weapon damage; the shaft provides the shove. Riveted Chainmail and Roman Legionary Helm are in Armor, War Trident is in Weapons, and Potion of Haste is in Misc. Do not restore the discarded phoenix helm.

- The visible ladder contains 18 ordered encounters: five random challengers and a fixed boss per stage, at progress 5/11/17. Wins alone advance it; defeats retry the same encounter. After completion, preserve uncapped wins and offer normal elite exhibition matches. Bosses have fixed named loadouts and owned belt copies, 1.2/1.3/1.25 rig scale, independent movement/attack modifiers and maximum health. Scale actual collision geometry, paired execution sockets and carry positions with the rendered actor. Boss AI prioritizes useful drinks before offensive belt throws and keeps normal interruption/consumption rules.
- Mouse aiming uses world coordinates through the gameplay camera. Preserve absolute A/D+Space hop intent when a cursor movement flips facing in the same frame; derive the forward/back clip after resolving that facing. Both hands retain the exact crossbow grip while pitching up/down and turning toward the cursor. Spawn bolts at the actual rendered muzzle and compensate gravity to reach the aim point; ranged AI targets the opponent body, including on the floor. Never add automatic hits, delete the crossbow on firing or allow arrow pickups.

- Weapon attack is its complete fixed raw damage (catalog version 2); subtract only the sum of worn helmet, chest and rear-hand shield armor, with a zero floor. No random/critical multipliers; only explicit learned Rage/Berserker/Superman/Counterstrike modifiers alter ordinary weapon damage. Keep armor arithmetic and wear thresholds internal. Landed weapon hits wear the contacted region (head to helmet; torso/limbs to body), including fully absorbed hits; broken copies fly off, disappear permanently and never become pickups. Keep wear during an active fight and re-equipping. Repair surviving armor at battle outcome, exit and reload; never restore a broken copy. Show subtle crack overlays after 55% wear and resolved numeric damage on weapon hits, without armor formulas. During the exclusive shop visit, inventory Sell trades one unequipped copy for half its purchase price, rounded down, without touching worn copies.

- Keep the front shoulder and upper-arm artwork permanently behind the torso in a separate `frontShoulder` pass. Front attacks, throws, parry counter-shoves and grapples may move only its forearm/hand artwork into the foreground. Both passes use identical IK shoulder transforms and fixed bones; the rear arm retains its existing foreground rendering.

- Melee quick attacks follow mouse elevation with a smooth, bounded pitch. Keep original strike arcs, fixed shoulder/bone transforms and exact paired weapon grips; reduce pitch at joint limits. Kicks, throws and paired executions retain their authored motions. The shop has Weapons (held weapons and belt throwables), Armor (helmets/chest/shields) and Misc (potions/other utilities) categories; preserve selected category and consumable quantity while purchasing/paging.

- Focus loss releases held/queued inputs without pausing combat or switching Auto Battle off. Alt/Meta shortcuts must not take manual control. Preserve the shared background clock: hidden timers and RAF callbacks must not double-step combat, discard throttled elapsed time, skip physical fixed-step contacts or leave timers alive after game destruction.

- New gladiators/reset have `skills: []`: only movement, stance, pickups, light armor and one one-handed melee weapon are basic. Use the centralized stable numeric skill catalog with Heavy Armor ID 18 and shared equipment/action/input checks. Only Shield Bash requires Shield Mastery and Counterstrike requires Parrying; Marksmanship does not also require Heavy Weapons. Enemies receive exactly the player's learned skill count and compatible gear and player AI receives no permission bypass. Preserve existing mechanics behind their skill gates.
- Every completed victory/defeat/draw persists one exclusive skill-or-shop branch with consumed/completed flags and the reward battle ID. Abandonment creates no branch. Boot/scene guards resume pending decisions/training/shop after reload; immediately learn exactly one of two saved random eligible skill offers or close exactly one shop visit. No free camp shop or camp sales. Show only learned skills in Gladiator; Camp has no Skills entry. Keep this read-only list distinct from post-battle learning. Save format 4 explicitly migrates older owned gear/gold/progression to zero advanced skills and unequips restricted gear without deleting ownership.

- Equipment has light/heavy classes; heavy helmets, body armor and Tower Shield require Heavy Armor. Keep Shield Bash ID 14 dormant while shields are passive. Auto Battle schedules a usable skill after 1–3 accepted normal attacks, with urgent defense/healing/recovery preserved. Superman Punch needs at least 175 × actor scale units of separation, checked in the engine.
- The optional hit-pause experiment starts at 200 ms in the arena, with live toggle and ±50 ms controls (the authorized exception to no combat buttons). Those controls must preserve Auto Battle and never queue attacks. Positive HP damage freezes both actors, except dual-wielding attackers freeze only the contacting hand. Keep experiment timing isolated and real-time; disabling clears pauses immediately. Facial pain stays below helmets and can animate during the pause.

- Shop's checkbox filters stock by item skill requirements, independently of ownership and occupied slots. Apply filtering before pagination; retain the flag through category, quantity, purchase and inventory transitions. Gladiator shows only learned skills in a compact wrapping list.
- Rage has a short brace/roar activation and red eyes plus an angry mouth throughout its real-time buff. Facial pain/helm occlusion still work and expiry restores the normal face. Rear shield carry reaches forward (+54, +42 from its shoulder); active drink/throw poses and their return blends temporarily override that carry. Always clear stale runtime prop flags after actions.
